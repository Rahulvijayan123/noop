## Selection rationale

- **Define (Set A, 36 files):** every migration that contains a `create function`, `alter function ... rename`, or `set schema` for a pinned RPC, plus its revoke/grant lines.
- **Projection target tables (Set B, 7 files):** migrations that `create table`/`alter table` the 13 streams resolved by `noop_projection_target` (`noop_hr_samples`, `noop_rr_intervals`, `noop_rr_packet_provenance`, `noop_standard_hr_receipts`, `noop_step_samples`, `noop_sleep_state_samples`, `noop_ppg_hr_samples`, `noop_events`, `noop_battery_samples`, `noop_spo2_samples`, `noop_skin_temp_samples`, `noop_resp_samples`, `noop_gravity_samples`) plus their RLS/provenance alters.
- **Helper functions the RPCs call (Set C, 2 files):** `noop_signal_windows` (raw object lane) and `scoring_lock_device` (hrv dependencies) are invoked by `noop_push_save_ack`/`noop_commit_object_receipt` and `noop_project_append_batch` respectively.
- **Core contract tables the RPCs read/write (Set D, 3 files):** `object_manifests` + device persistence, `noop_app_installations`, and `noop_object_copy_intents` are directly written by the pinned RPCs.
- **Scoped intake wrappers (Set E, 1 file):** `20260922130000_scoped_intake_admission.sql` defines `noop_claim_projection_debt_scoped`, `noop_claim_object_verification_scoped`, and the canary flow that calls the pinned RPCs.
- **Deliberately NOT pinned (98 migrations):** frwhoop base schema, settings/integrations, sleep storage, day-completeness, physiology calibration, RR measurement identity, fleet scheduler internals beyond the listed RPCs, history clock durability, bounded-recovery archive, and all other migrations unrelated to the pinned RPC surface.
