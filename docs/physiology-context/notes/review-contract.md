| RC-11 | §6.1 ("a missing activity interval is not sedentary time"), §13, V14 | H | CONFIRMED-DEFECT | `PCFunctional.swift:219` only adds `activity_evidence_unavailable` as a reason. | RC-02 | §7 (regime boundaries), §12.2, V04 | H | CONFIRMED-DEFECT | `PCEngine.swift:161` uses `deviceRegimeKey = registry.keys.sorted().first`. That is the lexicographically smallest hash among the day's measurement regimes, and the key includes the method id.
